Full Project Here InfoGraphic

This task's objective is to use a range of resources to research a communication technology, ascertain its history, workings, applications, effects, and possible future. Then, compile all of this information into a visual presentation. Making an infographic that demonstrates how the chosen communication technology fits into the communication technology model and effectively communicates an understanding of its working principle is another aspect of the endeavor.

In this project we were givin a choice to research any topic relating to technology and make a interesting infographic about what you learned about said topic.
