Hello, my name is Jeremiah Anderson and this is my website. Visitors to this site will find samples of my work, a summary of my academic and extracurricular involvement, and insights into the values and goals that guide my efforts. This platform also serves as a space to connect with others who share similar interests and to document my continued growth beyond Millersville. I look forward to using it as a tool for reflection, outreach, and professional development.
